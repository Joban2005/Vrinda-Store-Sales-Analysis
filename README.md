## Project Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's sales data to identify customer purchasing patterns, sales performance, order status, top-performing states, sales channels, and customer demographics.

The project uses Excel for data analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI development, and dashboard creation.

The dashboard was developed to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support marketing and sales decisions.

---

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze store sales data and answer key business questions such as:

- Which customer segment generates the most revenue?
- How do sales compare between men and women?
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- Which sales channels contribute the most orders?
- What is the order status distribution?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- Which sales channel generates the highest-value orders?

## Key Insights

### Customer Demographics

Women contribute a larger share of total sales compared with men, making female customers an important target segment for marketing campaigns.

### Geographic Performance

Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu are among the strongest-performing states based on sales.

### Sales Channels

Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart are major sales channels and contribute a significant proportion of overall orders and revenue.

### Age Group Revenue

The Adult customer segment generates the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups.

### Average Order Value

The overall Average Order Value is approximately **₹743.79**, meaning the average order generates around ₹744 in revenue.

### Channel AOV

Amazon has the highest Average Order Value among the analyzed channels at approximately **₹708.96**.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
2. Continue investing in high-performing sales channels such as Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart.
3. Develop targeted promotions for high-performing states.
4. Use customer demographic insights to create more personalized offers.
5. Monitor Average Order Value by channel to identify opportunities to increase basket size.
6. Use dashboard filters to regularly monitor changes in sales performance.
